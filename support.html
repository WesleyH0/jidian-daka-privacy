常见问题 / Frequently Asked Questions

  • 如何创建打卡项目：打开 App 后点击添加按钮，填写项目名称，并根据需要设置颜色、图标和提醒。
    How to create a habit item: Open the App, tap the add button, enter the habit name, and optionally set a color, icon, and reminder.
  • 如何完成今日打卡：在首页点击对应项目的打卡圆圈，即可完成当天打卡。
    How to complete today’s check-in: Tap the check-in circle for the corresponding habit item on the home screen to mark it as completed for today.
  • 如何添加打卡备注：你可以使用记录打卡功能，在完成打卡后添加当天备注，例如学习时长、运动内容或当天状态。
    How to add a check-in note: You can use the note check-in feature to add a note after completing a habit, such as study duration, workout details, or your daily status.
  • 如何设置提醒：在项目设置中开启本地提醒，并选择提醒时间。提醒会在你的设备本地生成。
    How to set reminders: Turn on local reminders in the habit item settings and choose a reminder time. Reminders are generated locally on your device.
  • 如何查看历史记录：你可以在日历回顾中查看每一天的打卡情况和备注内容。
    How to view history: You can view your daily check-in status and notes in the calendar history section.
  • 如何查看统计数据：你可以在统计或洞察页面查看近期完成趋势，例如近 7 天和近 30 天的完成情况。
    How to view statistics: You can view recent completion trends, such as the last 7 days and last 30 days, in the statistics or insights section.
  • 如何升级高级版：在 App 内点击升级入口，通过 App Store 内购解锁高级功能。
    How to upgrade to Premium: Tap the upgrade entry in the App and unlock premium features through App Store in-app purchase.
  • 如何恢复购买：如果你已经购买高级版，可以在升级页面点击恢复购买。
    How to restore purchases: If you have already purchased Premium, you can tap Restore Purchases on the upgrade page.
  • 如何导出数据：高级版用户可以在相关功能页面将打卡数据导出为 CSV 文件。
    How to export data: Premium users can export habit data as a CSV file from the relevant feature page.
  • 数据是否会上传到服务器：默认情况下，你的打卡项目、记录、备注和统计数据保存在本地设备中。我们不会要求注册账号，也不会将你的打卡内容上传到我们的服务器。
    Will my data be uploaded to a server? By default, your habit items, records, notes, and statistics are stored on your local device. We do not require account registration and do not upload your habit content to our own servers.
  • iCloud 备份或同步如何工作：如果你开启 iCloud 私有备份或同步，相关数据可能会通过你自己的 Apple iCloud 账户在你的设备之间同步。该过程由 Apple iCloud 提供支持。
    How does iCloud backup or sync work? If you enable private iCloud backup or sync, related data may be synced across your own devices through your Apple iCloud account. This process is provided by Apple iCloud.
